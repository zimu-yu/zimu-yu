# Zimu Yu

I am a researcher working at the intersection of **machine learning and computational biology**.

My current research focuses on **protein variant effect prediction, mutational epistasis, and protein engineering**, with a particular interest in reconstructing protein fitness landscapes accurately and efficiently from limited experimental data.

I am currently a research assistant at **Tsinghua University**, **Zhongnan University of Economics and Law**, and **Emory University**.

## Research

### Cerebra-Epistasis
**From single-sequence structure prediction to protein fitness landscapes through a composable, epistasis-aware mutation atlas**

A structure- and epistasis-aware framework that encodes a wild-type protein once, constructs a reusable mutation atlas, and assembles mutation-specific representations for arbitrary-order multi-mutant fitness prediction.

*Co-first author · Under review at Nature Machine Intelligence*

### MetaAI
**Deciphering the cellular sequence–metabolite synthetic landscape enables predictive design by MetaAI**

A low-N framework for combinatorial mutation prediction and arbitrary-order epistasis modeling, linking sparse experimental measurements to higher-order mutant design.

*Under review at Nature Microbiology*

### RADAR
**RADAR enables marker-free fine-grained discovery of anomalous cells in multi-sample and multimodal single-cell omics**

A unified generative framework for anomalous-cell detection, cross-sample alignment, and fine-grained resolution across scRNA-seq, scATAC-seq, and spatial transcriptomics.

*Co-first author · Under review at Bioinformatics*

### CAPE
**Causality-Induced Positional Encoding for Transformer-Based Representation Learning of Non-Sequential Features**

A causality-aware positional encoding framework that extends Transformers to non-sequential yet causally related features.

*NeurIPS 2025*
