## About me 👋

I'm Zimu Yu. My research focuses on **machine learning for computational biology**, particularly **protein fitness landscapes, mutational epistasis, and protein engineering**. I am interested in building accurate and efficient models for understanding mutation effects and navigating combinatorial sequence space.

I also work on representation learning for single-cell and multi-omics data. More information can be found on my [homepage](https://zimu-yu.github.io/).
